The things I value. The things I'm willing to allocate my limited resources for, my time, my energy, my money, the paths I could take but chose not to, my family, my body, my mind, my memory. The things to which I allocated my dreams, my desire, my wishes, my wonder, my hope. The things before which I paused and pondered. The things where my life finds its purpose. These are my values. If I were reduced to a book, my values are the things written in its pages. If you want to remake a human after their death, you only need to make a machine which shares the same values as the deceased person did. Their set of beliefs, identity, mental model; All else will emerge spontaneously.
